Gridlock Escape

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 19, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Gridlock Escape does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Game progress stays on your device. Optional analytics help Nocturnal Devs understand and improve the game.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Nocturnal Devs ("Nocturnal Devs," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Gridlock Escape mobile application on iOS or Android (the "App"). It applies only to Gridlock Escape and not to unrelated client projects or third-party websites.

2. Information handled by the App

Game data stored locally

The App stores level progress, active puzzle state, best move counts, coin balance, daily reward status, tutorial status, and preferences such as sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, analytics choice, and future ad preference in local app storage on your device. The local storage itself is not uploaded. If you allow Analytics, limited measurements derived from play, such as level and move counts, are sent as described below.

Optional Firebase Analytics data

If you allow analytics, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase. Google may automatically process:

  • a randomly generated app-instance identifier;
  • app and device information such as platform, app version, device model, operating-system version, language, and screen or session information;
  • general location derived from a masked IP address, not precise GPS location;
  • app lifecycle information such as first open, sessions, and screen views;
  • gameplay events such as opening the game, starting or completing a level, move counts, hints, undo or restart use, reward interactions, and changes to the future ad preference; and
  • on Android, the device Advertising ID when it is available to the Analytics SDK.

We do not set a user ID, create a player account, or send your name, email address, phone number, messages, or other user-entered content to Firebase Analytics.

3. How we use information

We use local game data to save your progress and preferences. If you allow analytics, we use Analytics data to understand which platforms and app versions are being used, measure engagement with levels and features, identify usability problems, balance gameplay, and improve reliability and design.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use Gridlock Escape Analytics data to make decisions about employment, credit, insurance, housing, or other sensitive eligibility matters.

4. Analytics choice and controls

The App asks whether you want to allow optional analytics. Analytics remains disabled unless you choose to allow it. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy & data. Turning analytics off stops future Analytics collection and resets the App's current local Analytics identifier.

You can erase locally saved gameplay data through Settings > Reset progress. Uninstalling the App also removes its local game data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

5. Service providers and disclosures

If you allow analytics, Google LLC and its subprocessors process Analytics data to provide Firebase and Google Analytics services to us. Google states that Firebase data is encrypted in transit. Learn more from Google's Firebase privacy and security information and Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or the public, investigate fraud or security incidents, enforce our rights, or complete a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of business assets. Any successor would remain subject to this policy for information collected under it unless you are notified otherwise.

6. Advertising

No advertising network is currently integrated into Gridlock Escape. A local ad preference may appear in the App for planned features, but it is not presently sent to an advertising provider. If advertising is introduced, we will update this policy and the applicable App Store and Google Play disclosures before using information for that purpose.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset progress, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. If you allow analytics, we retain event-level Analytics data for no longer than 14 months unless a shorter period is required, while aggregated or de-identified reporting may be retained longer. Google may retain information as described in its terms and policies.

Because the App has no accounts and we do not attach Analytics data to your name or contact details, we generally cannot identify a particular Analytics record as belonging to you. You may still contact us with an access or deletion request, and we will use available tools to respond as required by applicable law.

8. International processing and security

Nocturnal Devs is based in Canada. Google and its subprocessors may process Analytics data in Canada, the United States, and other countries where they operate. Those countries may have privacy laws different from the laws where you live.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, to withdraw consent, or to complain to a privacy regulator. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

The fastest controls are available directly in the App. For any other request, contact us using the details below. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request, but we will not ask for more than reasonably necessary.

10. Children's privacy

Gridlock Escape is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. The App does not offer accounts or ask users to submit names or contact information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, our service providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the date above. If a change materially affects your choices, we will provide an additional notice in the App where appropriate.

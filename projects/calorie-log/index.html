Nutrition tracking / iOS + Android

Calorie Log AI tracker.

AI-assisted calorie tracking for local-first food logging. Capture meals with a photo, scan a barcode or enter food manually, then follow calories, macros and progress over time.

Available on iOS + Android

Flexible meal logging

Track food in the way that fits.

Calorie Log brings common nutrition workflows into one place while keeping the primary meal history and profile local to the device.

01 / Camera

Photo nutrition estimates

Send a meal photo for AI-assisted estimates of calories, macros and nutrition details without building every entry by hand.

02 / Barcode

Packaged food lookup

Scan a barcode and retrieve available product and nutrition information from Open Food Facts.

03 / Goals

Personal calorie targets

Set a daily target using profile and activity information, then compare meal totals with that goal.

04 / Trends

Progress over time

Review daily summaries, weekly trends, 30-day views, streaks and meal patterns from the app dashboard.

05 / Health

Optional health connections

With permission, read calorie-burn information from Apple HealthKit or Android Health Connect for consumed-versus-burned views.

06 / Reuse

Favourites and manual entry

Save repeat meals for faster logging, or enter and adjust meal details yourself whenever that is more useful.

Local-first by design

A clearer view of everyday nutrition.

Meal estimates help reduce logging friction, while manual controls keep the final record in the user’s hands.

  1. 01

    Set a personal target

    Enter the profile and activity details used to establish an everyday calorie goal.

  2. 02

    Capture each meal

    Choose a photo, barcode or manual entry, then review the nutrition estimate before relying on it.

  3. 03

    Review the pattern

    Compare the day with your goal and use longer-term summaries to understand recurring habits.

Nutrition context: AI and barcode results are estimates and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Calorie Log is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Although the app is local-first, selected features send limited data to external providers when used.

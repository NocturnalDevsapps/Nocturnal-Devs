Work records without the spreadsheet

Keep the shift in view.

Clock In Pro combines day-to-day time capture with the overtime, deductions and summaries that basic timer apps often leave out.

01 / Time

Clock in, pause and finish

Record the start and end of a shift, pause for breaks, and edit or remove an entry when a correction is needed.

02 / Overtime

Adapt to your rules

Configure overtime settings and review regular and overtime hours across weekly and monthly summaries.

03 / Earnings

Estimate net pay

Add pay details, taxes and custom deductions to produce a clearer take-home-pay estimate.

04 / Profiles

Separate multiple jobs

Create distinct work profiles, switch between jobs and keep their records and summaries apart.

05 / Reports

See and export the record

Use charts to compare work patterns, then export reports for personal records, payroll review or invoicing.

06 / Access

Multilingual and local-first

Switch the interface language and use the core time-tracking workflow without an account or mandatory cloud sync.

Designed for changing schedules

From first clock-in to clearer records.

Useful for shift workers, healthcare and hospitality staff, drivers, consultants, students, interns and anyone balancing more than one job.

  1. 01

    Configure the job

    Set work, pay, overtime, deduction, language and currency preferences for the profile.

  2. 02

    Track the shift

    Clock in, pause when needed and clock out, with the option to correct entries afterward.

  3. 03

    Review and export

    Check regular hours, overtime and estimated earnings, then export a report when you need a portable record.

Pay context: Hours, overtime, deductions and take-home pay are user-configured estimates, not payroll, tax or legal advice. Verify important totals against employer, payroll and tax records. Core tracking works offline; app-store services, ads, analytics and updates may use an internet connection.

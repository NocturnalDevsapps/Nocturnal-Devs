How to play / One way out

Read the grid. Open the lane.

The goal is simple. Getting there takes a plan.

  1. 01

    Study the traffic

    Find what blocks the red escape car and which vehicles have room to move in their lanes.

  2. 02

    Slide with purpose

    Move vehicles forward or backward to create space without trapping the next move.

  3. 03

    Clear the exit

    Open a route for the red car, escape the grid and compare your move count with par.

Game systems / Built for replay

More ways to solve smarter.

01 / Campaign

Progressive puzzle path

Unlock levels as you clear the campaign, then revisit any open puzzle from level select.

02 / Score

Par, stars and best runs

Track moves, chase a one-to-three-star result and try to beat your personal best.

03 / Assist

Hints, undo and restart

Use a solver-assisted next-move hint, step back with undo or reset the board and try again.

04 / Resume

Pick up where you left off

Pause a puzzle and resume the current board without rebuilding your move sequence.

05 / Local

Core campaign offline

Play the bundled campaign without an account. Game progress is stored locally on your device.

06 / Comfort

Play it your way

Adjust sound, music, haptics and reduced motion from the game settings.

Directory experience

From “near me” to what matters.

The directory is designed to answer more than where a business is located. It surfaces the details people often need before choosing a service.

01 / Find

Search near a location

Browse by state and city, search by business or service, or use browser location to surface nearby options.

02 / Compare

Check booking signals

Compare known prices, durations, treatment format, scalp-analysis availability, drying details and online-booking options when sources support them.

03 / Verify

Follow the evidence

Use verification labels, source links, last-checked dates and a documented corrections process to judge how current each listing may be.

Confirm before booking. Menus, prices, hours and practitioner qualifications can change. Head Spa Scout is an independent directory, not a medical provider or booking marketplace.

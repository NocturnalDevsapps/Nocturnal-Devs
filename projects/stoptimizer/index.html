Stoptimizer route planner.

Optimize every stop before the road gets busy. Stoptimizer turns a list of addresses into a clearer stop order so delivery drivers, couriers and field-service teams can spend less time backtracking.

Built for multi-stop days

From address list to next stop.

The workflow stays focused: collect the stops, set any route constraints, optimize the sequence and continue in your preferred navigation app.

01 / Capture

Add stops quickly

Type an address or use voice entry, then review its matching marker on the map.

02 / Order

Optimize the sequence

Reorder a multi-stop route while keeping a required first or last stop when the day calls for it.

03 / Navigate

Move into navigation

Follow the optimized stop list and launch navigation for the next address when you are ready to drive.

04 / Capacity

Scale up route days

The official site currently lists 7 stops per route on the free tier and larger limits with Stoptimizer Pro.

05 / Context

Plan around changing roads

Review routing and available traffic information before setting out, with alternate-route support on the Pro plan.

06 / Audience

Made for work on the move

A focused workflow

Plan first. Drive second.

Stoptimizer supports route planning; it does not replace road signs, traffic laws or a driver’s judgement.

  1. 01

    Build the stop list

    Add the addresses you need to visit and confirm the locations shown on the map.

  2. 02

    Choose route constraints

    Set a first or final stop when the route has a fixed start, handoff or return point.

  3. 03

    Optimize and review

    Generate a revised stop sequence, check it against the day’s conditions and open navigation when parked and ready.

Route safety: Maps, traffic, geocoding and travel-time information can be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Verify every route, obey posted conditions and never interact with the app while driving.

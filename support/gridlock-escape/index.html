FAQ / Quick answers

Gridlock Escape support FAQ.

Which platforms support Gridlock Escape?

Gridlock Escape is available now on iOS and Android. Visit the product page for the official App Store and Google Play download links.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Gridlock Escape does not require an account, and game progress is stored on your device.

How does analytics work?

Firebase Analytics runs automatically for players 16 and older and remains disabled for ages 13–15. The privacy policy explains the age protections and data handling.

How do I report a problem?

Email Nocturnal Devs with the device, operating system, app version, steps that led to the problem and a screenshot when helpful.

Where can I read the Gridlock Escape privacy policy?

The Gridlock Escape privacy policy is publicly available on the Nocturnal Devs website.
